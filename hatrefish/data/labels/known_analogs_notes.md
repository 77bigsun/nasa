# Known Mars analog sites: source notes (draft, unverified)

Each line: site_id: source title. Justification. Coordinates marked "approx" are estimated centres of the studied area, not taken from the source.

## Confirmed rows (28)

- atacama_yungay: Navarro-Gonzalez et al. 2003, "Mars-like soils in the Atacama Desert, Chile, and the dry limit of microbial life", Science 302. The Yungay hyperarid core soils are called Mars-like, with organics too scarce for Viking-type instruments to detect. (Coordinates from the task.)
- mcmurdo_dry_valleys: Tamppari et al. 2012, "Effects of extreme cold and aridity on soils and habitability: McMurdo Dry Valleys as an analogue for the Mars Phoenix landing site", Antarctic Science 24. The abstract calls the Dry Valleys "excellent analogues for the Martian northern plains" (lower Taylor Valley and University Valley). (Coordinates from the task; regional centre.)
- beacon_valley: Schorghofer 2005, Geophysical Research Letters (doi 10.1029/2005GL023881), on the survival of buried ground ice in Beacon Valley. Water vapour slows the ice's sublimation, and the paper proposes the same process explains ground ice persisting at Mars's high latitudes. (Coordinates approx.)
- haughton_crater: NASA Science, "10 Things to Know About Planetary Analogs". The Haughton-Mars Project at Haughton Crater, Devon Island, uses barren terrain, freezing temperatures and isolation that "mimic conditions on Mars". (Coordinates from the task.)
- axel_heiberg_lost_hammer: Ward and Pollard 2018, "A hydrohalite spring deposit in the Canadian high Arctic: A potential Mars analogue", EPSL. A perennial cold saline spring flowing through permafrost precipitates hydrohalite and is proposed as a Mars analogue. (Coordinates approx.)
- svalbard_bockfjorden: Blake, Treiman et al. 2011, "Carbonate Cements from the Sverrefjell and Sigurdfjell Volcanoes, Svalbard Norway: Analogs for Martian Carbonates", LPSC 42 (NASA NTRS). The globules are described as "the best terrestrial analogs to the carbonate globules in the Mars meteorite ALH84001" (AMASE field site). (Coordinates approx; Sverrefjell.)
- mdrs_hanksville: Clarke and Stoker 2011, "Concretions in exhumed and inverted channels near Hanksville, Utah: implications for Mars", International Journal of Astrobiology. The area near the Mars Desert Research Station is an analogue for exhumed and inverted Martian channels, with protected floodplain clays. (Coordinates from the task.)
- utah_navajo_concretions: Chan et al. 2004, "A possible terrestrial analogue for haematite concretions on Mars", Nature 429. Haematite concretions in the Jurassic Navajo Sandstone of southern Utah closely resemble the Meridiani "blueberries" found by Opportunity. (Coordinates approx; the paper covers a broad southern Utah outcrop belt, so the radius is 50 km.)
- pilot_valley_utah: NASA Science "Planetary Analog Explorer" (pair "Looking for Life: Utah and Mars"). The salts of Utah's Pilot Valley paleolake are studied as an analog for perchlorate-bearing environments on Mars. (Coordinates approx.)
- black_point_lava_flow: ASU News, "Arizona stands in for the Moon and Mars" (Desert RATS). The site is described as having "lunar and Martian analog geomorphology and geology" and hosts NASA Desert RATS field tests. Note that most Black Point sources stress lunar rather than Mars use. (Coordinates from the task.)
- death_valley_badwater: Baldridge, Farmer and Moersch 2004, "Mars remote-sensing analog studies in the Badwater Basin, Death Valley, California", JGR Planets 109. Badwater evaporites (sulfates and carbonates) were used to test what TES and THEMIS can detect on Mars. NASA's Planetary Analog Explorer also pairs Death Valley with Utopia Planitia. (Coordinates approx.)
- mojave_lavic_lake: Arvidson et al. 1998, "Rocky 7 prototype Mars rover field geology experiments: 1. Lavic Lake and Sunshine volcanic field, California", JGR Planets. Experiments with the Rocky 7 Mars rover prototype in the Mojave tested how to run long rover traverses on Mars. (Coordinates approx; Lavic Lake.)
- white_sands_lake_lucero: Chavdarian and Sumner 2006, "Cracks and fins in sulfate sand...", Geology 34. Gypsum dunes at White Sands "provide an excellent analog to sulfate-rich eolian outcrops on Meridiani Planum". (Coordinates approx; between the dune field and Lake Lucero.)
- pavilion_lake: NASA, "Pavilion Lake, Canada" (Analog Field Testing). The exploration methods developed for the lake's microbialites "are used to plan for exploration of the Moon, Mars, and asteroids". See also Lim et al. 2011, GSA Special Paper 483. (Coordinates approx.)
- lake_salda: NASA Earth Observatory, "Jez like Mars". Lake Salda shares mineralogy and geology with Jezero crater and is "the only known lake on Earth that contains the carbonates and depositional features" like Jezero's. (Coordinates from the NASA EO map link.)
- rio_tinto: Fernandez-Remolar et al. 2005, Earth and Planetary Science Letters 240 (doi 10.1016/j.epsl.2005.09.043), on the Rio Tinto basin and Meridiani Planum. Its sulfate- and hematite-rich deposits are a partial environmental analog for Meridiani outcrop rocks. (Coordinates approx; headwaters near Minas de Riotinto.)
- ries_crater: Sturm et al. 2013, "The Ries impact, a double-layer rampart crater on Earth", Geology 41. The Ries ejecta resemble double-layered ejecta craters on Mars. A NASA Astrobiology highlight also notes that the Ries crater-lake deposits bear similarities to Jezero. (Coordinates approx; crater centre.)
- askja_vikrahraun: Blasizzo et al. 2022, "Vikrahraun - the 1961 basaltic lava flow eruption at Askja, Iceland: morphology, geochemistry, and planetary analogs", Earth Planets and Space 74. The flow is presented as a planetary lava-flow analog. NASA's Planetary Analog Explorer also uses the Askja/Vikursundar area as an analog for Mars ripples. (Coordinates from the task.)
- lanzarote: ESA, "Lanzarote" (2016, PANGAEA astronaut geology training). ESA states that "The Lanzarote national park is in many ways similar to Mars". The volcanic terrain and lava tubes are used for ESA Mars-analogue training. (Coordinates approx; island centre.)
- mauna_kea_palagonite: Morris, Graff and Mertzman 2001, "Phyllosilicate-poor palagonitic dust from Mauna Kea Volcano (Hawaii): A mineralogical analogue for magnetic Martian dust?", JGR. Dust from the upper slopes of Mauna Kea is a spectral and magnetic analogue for Martian bright-region dust. NASA also ran ISRU field tests on Mauna Kea in 2008 and 2010 (NTRS 20100021362). (Coordinates approx.)
- hiseas_mauna_loa: University of Hawaii News 2017, "Mission V crew enters HI-SEAS Mars simulation habitat on Mauna Loa". HI-SEAS is a NASA-funded Mars-simulation habitat at about 8,200 ft on Mauna Loa. (Coordinates approx. The page appeared in search results, but a direct fetch was refused by the server.)
- kau_desert_kilauea: Chemtob et al. 2010, "Silica coatings on the 1974 Kilauea flow: new SEM and SIMS results and implications for Mars", JGR Planets. These acid-sulfate silica coatings in the Ka'u Desert resemble the high-silica deposits at Gusev crater; related work calls the Ka'u Desert "a Mars analog terrain". (Coordinates approx.)
- lonar_crater: Hagerty and Newsom 2003, "Hydrothermal alteration at the Lonar Lake impact structure, India: Implications for impact cratering on Mars", MAPS 38. This rare basalt-hosted crater is an analogue for similarly sized Martian craters and their hydrothermal systems. (Coordinates approx; crater centre.)
- kachchh_matanumadh: Bhattacharya et al. 2016, "Jarosite occurrence in the Deccan Volcanic Province of Kachchh, western India: Spectroscopic studies on a Martian analog locality", JGR Planets 121. Jarosite with kaolinite over Fe/Mg smectites in altered basalt resembles Martian jarosite localities. (Coordinates approx.)
- qaidam_dalangtan: 2018 (authors not checked), "Dalangtan Saline Playa in a Hyperarid Region on Tibet Plateau: I. Evolution and Environments", Astrobiology 18. The playa is proposed "as a potential Mars analog site" because of its sulfate and chloride salts and its low-pressure, high-UV, hyperarid climate. (Coordinates approx; the paper gives the area as 38°0'-38°40'N, 91°10'-92°10'E.)
- dallol: Cavalazzi et al. 2019, "The Dallol Geothermal Area, Northern Afar (Ethiopia) - An Exceptional Planetary Field Analog on Earth", Astrobiology 19. Its hydrothermalism and extensive acidic sulfate deposits resemble past hydrothermal settings on Mars. (Coordinates approx.)
- dhofar_amadee18: Austrian Space Forum (OeWF), "AMADEE-18 Mars Analog Mission". The February 2018 Mars analog field simulation in Dhofar, Oman, chose terrain that has "a resemblance to various Mars surface features". (Coordinates approx. The source gives only "Dhofar, near Salalah"; press reports place the camp on the outskirts of Marmul.)
- pilbara_dresser: NASA/JPL, "Mars scientists investigate ancient life in Australia" (2019). Mars 2020 and ExoMars team members visited the Dresser Formation stromatolites to learn what to look for at Jezero and Oxia Planum. (Coordinates approx; North Pole Dome.)

## Unconfirmed / dropped

- Namib Desert (Namibia): only press articles (Mail & Guardian) and a Titan-analog note (Lorenz 2016) turned up. No peer-reviewed or NASA/ESA page calling the Namib a Mars analog was found, so it was dropped.
- Jurassic Coast (UK): no source found describing it as a Mars analog. Dropped.
- Utah Green River / San Rafael Swell: not confirmed as a separate site. The Clarke and Stoker (2011) MDRS paper places its study area on the edge of the San Rafael Swell, so this area is covered by the mdrs_hanksville row instead of a duplicate.
- Kilauea (main volcano): NASA's Planetary Analog Explorer pairs Kilauea with the Moon, not Mars. Only the Ka'u Desert alteration work, which is Mars-specific, was kept.
- Mauna Kea ISRU tests (2008 and 2010): NASA NTRS reports describe these tests as lunar ISRU. The Mars-oriented MARCO POLO demo there was only planned. The row relies on the Morris 2001 Mars dust analog paper, not on the ISRU tests.
- Antarctic University Valley: covered by the McMurdo Dry Valleys row (Tamppari 2012) instead of a separate row.
